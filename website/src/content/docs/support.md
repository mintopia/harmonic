Harmonic is free and open source, built and maintained in spare time. If it's
working through your backlog and you'd like to say thanks, you can sponsor its
development on GitHub.

<p><a class="sponsor-btn" href="https://github.com/sponsors/mintopia">Sponsor on GitHub</a></p>

One-off and monthly sponsorships both help, and so does
[starring the repository](https://github.com/mintopia/harmonic) or
[reporting a bug](https://github.com/mintopia/harmonic/issues).
